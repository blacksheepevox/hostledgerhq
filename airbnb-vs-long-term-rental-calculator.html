How this calculator works

Short-term: booked nights = nights available × occupancy %; stays = booked nights ÷ average stay length. Revenue is lodging plus cleaning fees charged; the platform fee applies to that revenue. Subtract platform fees, cleaning costs you pay, STR-only monthly costs and shared fixed costs to get STR net. Long-term: effective rent = monthly rent after vacancy; subtract management % (on effective rent), LTR-only costs and the same shared fixed costs. Difference is STR net minus LTR net (positive means STR wins). Break-even STR occupancy is the occupancy where STR net equals LTR net, using contribution per booked night after platform and cleaning. Percents are clamped 0–100; money inputs are clamped at zero; average stay is at least one night; nights available defaults to 30 if blank. Negative nets are allowed. Occupancy tax is left out on purpose.

When each strategy fits

Short-term can earn more in tourist or event markets with strong ADR and manageable turnovers, but it needs more active work, higher wear, platform fees and often local permits or primary-residence rules. Long-term is usually steadier cash flow with less day-to-day work, at the cost of lower upside and vacancy between tenants. Some owners run STR in peak season and switch to medium- or long-term leases in the off-season. Insurance, HOA rules and city caps can rule STR out entirely even when the spreadsheet favors it. FAQ

How is short-term different from long-term rental income?

Short-term (Airbnb, VRBO, direct) is usually priced per night with occupancy, cleaning fees, platform fees and higher operating costs. Long-term is a monthly lease with vacancy and optional property management. This calculator puts both on the same monthly and annual net so you can see which strategy wins at your numbers.

Why is occupancy tax left out?

In many markets the platform collects and remits occupancy or lodging tax for you, so it never hits your net. Where you remit it yourself, treat it as an extra STR cost or use the short-term rental profit calculator, which models host remittance. Rules vary by city and state—check yours.

What counts as shared fixed costs?

Costs you pay either way: mortgage principal and interest, property taxes, insurance and HOA. Enter them once so the comparison is fair. STR-only costs (internet, supplies, furnishing amortization, higher utilities) and LTR-only costs (landlord-paid utilities you would not pay as an STR host) sit in their own fields.

What does break-even STR occupancy mean?

It is the occupancy rate at which short-term monthly net equals long-term monthly net. Above it, STR wins; below it, LTR wins (when each booked night still contributes positively after fees and cleaning). If contribution per night is negative, break-even is not reachable.

Does this include local STR permits or HOA bans?

No. Many cities require short-term rental permits, caps or primary-residence rules, and some HOAs ban STR entirely. A higher STR net on paper is useless if you cannot operate legally. Confirm regulations and insurance before you switch.

Is my data saved or sent anywhere?

No. The math runs in your browser. Nothing is stored or sent, and refreshing the page resets the defaults.
