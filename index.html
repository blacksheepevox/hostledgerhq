A ledger, not a platform

HostLedger is a small catalog of spreadsheets for people who run a cabin and a loft, or a duplex and a single-family rental, and still reconcile in a sheet. Each file does one job. You type in what actually happened (the fee your channel charged, the rent that actually landed) and the dashboard does the math. No bank sync, no channel API, no login. A file you can hand to your bookkeeper.

Who it's for, and who it isn't

Good fit

  • Hosts with 1–4 short-term rentals who feel shoulder season in the numbers
  • Duplex and small multifamily landlords (1–10 units) who reconcile in a spreadsheet
  • Operators who want files they own and can hand to a bookkeeper, not another login

Not a fit

  • Portfolios that need automatic booking-channel sync, bank feeds, or team permissions
  • Anyone looking for tax filing, tax advice, or a CPA replacement
  • Hotels and large portfolios

FAQ

Is HostLedger tax software?

No. These are spreadsheets for running and tracking your properties: rent, profit, occupancy, logs. They do not file taxes or give tax advice. You or your tax professional decide what goes on a return.

Do they work in Google Sheets and Excel?

Yes. Every tool is a macro-free .xlsx file that opens in Excel. In Google Sheets, open the file and use File → Make a copy, then work in your copy.

What is in each download?

A blank template, a sample workbook filled with fictional data so you can see how it works, and a one-page quick-start PDF with the fill order.

How do I buy, and what about refunds?

All purchases happen on Etsy in the HostLedgerHQ shop. Etsy handles checkout and the instant download. Refund requests and questions go through Etsy too; just message the shop there.

Are the calculators really free?

Yes. They run entirely in your browser. No signup, no email, nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Why not just use a free template?

Free templates are often built around tax-form categories. HostLedger is built around running a small property week to week: seasonal pricing, rent due vs collected, deposits held, and the ops logs you open mid-week. If a free template already does what you need, use it.
